Zhixuan Liu 柳志轩

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Expected graduation: June 2027. Open to 2027 full-time and research internship opportunities in LLM post-training, alignment, and efficient inference.

I work on large language model training and inference, with a focus on safe distillation, inference-time alignment, efficient decoding, and search-based generation. I conduct part of my Ph.D. research through a joint training arrangement at Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory.

My research often starts from a question about model behavior and continues through method design, systems implementation, and large-model evaluation.

Selected research

Subliminal Learning as Trait-Direction Drift: A Mechanism and Targeted Control under SFT Distillation

NeurIPS 2026 Poster · First author

We explain latent-trait transfer under SFT distillation and reduce malicious-response transfer from 29.55% to 6.45% with probe-space corridor regularization.

Project pagearXivPDFBibTeX

Decoupled Contrastive Decoding via Expert-Aligned Drafting

EMNLP 2026 Main Conference · First author

DCD removes the amateur model from the serial proposal path while preserving the original contrastive-decoding distribution, reaching 1.65–1.95× average speedups in 8B settings and about 2× at 70B.

Project pagearXivPDFCodeBibTeX

Inference-Time Language Model Alignment via Integrated Value Guidance

EMNLP 2024 Findings · Co-first author, first-listed

IVG combines token-level implicit values with chunk-level search to align frozen target LLMs purely at inference time.

Project pagePaperarXivCodeBibTeX

Weak-to-Strong Search: Align Large Language Models via Searching over Small Language Models

NeurIPS 2024 · Co-first author, second-listed

We use small-model preference differences as dense rewards and values for chunk-level search over stronger frozen LLMs, including cross-tokenizer and black-box settings.

PaperarXivCodeBibTeX

A Neural-Symbolic Approach to Natural Language Understanding

EMNLP 2022 Findings · First author

A Mixture-of-Experts processor combines neural prediction with program generation and symbolic execution for numerical question answering and natural language inference.
