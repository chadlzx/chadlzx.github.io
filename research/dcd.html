Decoupled Contrastive Decoding via Expert-Aligned Drafting

DCD removes the amateur model from the serial proposal path, then restores the exact contrastive-decoding target during parallel verification.

01

Research question

Can contrastive decoding keep its target distribution while eliminating the expensive serial amateur proposal?

System bottleneck

The amateur model is useful for contrastive scoring, but a poor choice for the latency-critical proposal path.

A

Correctness

Verification must reproduce the original contrastive distribution—not an approximation to it.

B

Latency

Proposal latency sits on the critical path and directly limits wall-clock throughput.

C

Systems

The runtime must coordinate expert, amateur, and drafter states without cache divergence.
