Subliminal Learning as Trait-Direction Drift

Research question

Why can a teacher transmit a latent behavioral trait through semantically unrelated SFT data, and how can that transfer be suppressed without degrading the intended distillation task?

Core method

We explain subliminal learning as cumulative drift along a trait direction. Under a low-rank logit-linear approximation, samples with a larger teacher preference gap induce stronger trait-aligned SFT updates. A calibrated trait probe then defines a corridor in representation space, and probe-space corridor regularization constrains the distillation trajectory before the drift becomes observable behavior.

Key results

  • Reduced malicious-response transfer from 29.55% to 6.47%.
  • Consistently suppressed transfer across multiple animal-preference settings with Qwen-7B models.
  • Preserved performance on the intended distillation task while targeting the trait direction.
