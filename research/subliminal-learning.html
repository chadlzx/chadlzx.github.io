Subliminal Learning as Trait-Direction Drift: A Mechanism and Targeted Control under SFT Distillation

A mechanistic account of how latent traits cross an apparently unrelated SFT channel—and a targeted intervention that constrains the drift before it becomes behavior.

01

Research question

Why can a teacher transmit a latent behavioral trait through semantically unrelated SFT data?

Central tension

The training outputs do not mention the trait, yet the student can still inherit it. Surface semantics therefore cannot explain the transfer channel.

A

Mechanism

Identify the learning signal that survives inside unrelated teacher-generated data.

B

Dynamics

Explain how weak per-example signals accumulate into an observable behavioral shift.

C

Control

Suppress only the unwanted trait direction while preserving the intended SFT task.

02

Core method

MechanismTrait signals accumulate as directional drift
1

Unrelated outputs

The teacher generates data that is semantically disconnected from the latent trait.

2

Preference gap

The outputs still carry a small teacher preference gap along the target trait.

3

SFT updates

Larger gaps induce stronger trait-aligned parameter updates under a local approximation.

4

Trait drift

Repeated updates accumulate until the hidden direction appears as student behavior.

Key prediction

Examples with a larger teacher preference gap should produce stronger trait transfer.

Targeted controlProbe-space corridor regularization
1

Calibrate a probe

Learn a readout that tracks movement along the unwanted trait direction.

2

Define a corridor

Specify an allowed region around the safe representation trajectory.

3

Constrain drift

Penalize SFT updates that leave the corridor while retaining task learning.
